# HIST-REVIEW-1 Longitudinal Ecology Review Completion Report

## Objective
Deliver bounded interpretability tooling for longitudinal ecology outputs from HIST-DENSITY runs with observational-only semantics.

## Methodology
- Construct deterministic summary builders over OPS-HIST-7 style saturation payloads.
- Derive bounded continuity, recurrence, morphology, saturation, and temporal evolution reviews.
- Emit lightweight JSON and Markdown artifacts under `reports/hist_review1/`.

## Review Dimensions
- Continuity review
- Recurrence ecology review
- Morphology review
- Saturation review
- Temporal evolution review
- Operator interpretability helper panels

## Boundedness Strategy
- Fixed top-k helper lists.
- Fixed narrative and warning ceilings.
- Deterministic sorting for all categorical summaries.
- Lightweight markdown/json only, no graph/dashboard generation.

## Interpretability Strategy
- Human-readable observational narratives.
- Ratio-based concentration/diversity/continuity metrics.
- Cluster and sparsity summaries for quick operator inspection.

## Governance Posture
Certified observational-only outputs with explicit prohibitions on prediction/trading logic, replay activation, topology activation, autonomous orchestration, and cognition persistence.

## Operational Recommendations
- Use HIST-REVIEW-1 artifacts as post-run operator review attachments.
- Compare outputs across repeated HIST-DENSITY waves to inspect recurrence and continuity drift.

## Next-Phase Guidance
- Increase longitudinal artifact depth while preserving deterministic and bounded review behavior.
- Keep semantics observational and defer cognition-layer expansion.
