# LR6-EXP7 — Replay Ecology Interestingness Scoring

## Objective
Implement deterministic bounded interestingness scoring for LR6-EXP6A comparison outputs to prioritize structurally meaningful replay ecology change.

## Methodology
- Normalize LR6-EXP6A comparison structures into stable domain sections.
- Score seven domains with deterministic additive formulas.
- Bound all lists and score outputs.
- Build ranked and low-information views for operator prioritization.

## Interestingness Philosophy
Interestingness emphasizes replay novelty, replay recurrence, contradiction persistence, propagation bridge emergence, semantic gravity movement, cross-cluster coupling change, and saturation pressure significance when evidence support exists.

## Scoring Domain Design
1. Ecology state interestingness
2. Replay drift interestingness
3. Propagation interestingness
4. Contradiction interestingness
5. Saturation/monoculture interestingness
6. Ecosystem interaction interestingness
7. Entity/cluster attribution interestingness

## Scoring Band Design
- 0.00–0.24: low_information
- 0.25–0.49: routine_change
- 0.50–0.74: notable_ecological_change
- 0.75–1.00: high_interestingness_ecological_shift

## Evidence Support Design
Each scored item carries bounded evidence references. Weak or missing evidence sections automatically contribute caveats and lower interestingness confidence.

## Penalty Design
- Saturation pressure penalty applies when saturation concentration rises without replay novelty support.
- Monoculture penalty applies when cross-cluster coupling breadth collapses.

## Ranked Output Design
Composite output includes ranked interesting ecological changes and separated low-information changes for attention prioritization.

## Dashboard Payload Design
Dashboard payload includes scoring metadata, domain scores, ranked changes, top drivers, evidence quality band, saturation/monoculture penalty notes, caveats, and next observation priorities.

## Caveats
- Observational interestingness only.
- Evidence sparsity lowers confidence.
- Bounded lists may omit lower-priority details.

## Next Observation Priorities

- Track semantic gravity movement under saturation pressure.
- Observe replay novelty vs replay recurrence across cross-cluster coupling.
