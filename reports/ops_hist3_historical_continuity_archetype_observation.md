# OPS-HIST-3 Historical Continuity Compression & Archetype Observation

## Objective
Compress repeated historical continuity structures from OPS-HIST-2 into deterministic descriptive archetypes.

## Relationship to OPS-HIST-1 and OPS-HIST-2
OPS-HIST-1 remains the deterministic snapshot foundation. OPS-HIST-2 remains the continuity intelligence layer. OPS-HIST-3 consumes OPS-HIST-2 continuity intelligence outputs and compresses repeated continuity patterns into bounded archetype observations.

## Continuity Compression Design
OPS-HIST-3 maps posture and continuity metric trajectories to deterministic archetype labels with stable ordering and explicit snapshot coverage.

## Archetype Label Design
Dimension labels are descriptive-only for posture, fragmentation, resilience, sector concentration, volatility, valuation dispersion, normalization quality, fallback usage, plus a composite continuity archetype.

## Evidence Record Design
Each archetype includes deterministic evidence records with source/schema versions, reviewed snapshot count, window dates, evidence fields used, observed summaries, rationale, and governance metadata.

## Streamlit Payload Design
Frontend-independent payloads include scorecard panel, dimension table, composite/stable/mixed/fragile panels, evidence table, and governance panel.

## Canonical Table Payload Design
Flat deterministic rows are emitted for archetype rows, evidence rows, composite rows, count rows, dimension rows, and governance rows.

## Governance Certification
OPS-HIST-3 is observational-only, historical-only, descriptive-only, local JSON-only, and excludes prediction/trading/replay/topology/graph/orchestration/streaming activation.

## Deterministic Test Summary
Deterministic tests validate fail-closed behavior, schema/version stability, label stability, payload key stability, markdown stability, and governance boundary presence.

## Explicit Forbidden Architecture Boundaries
No prediction, no forecasting, no trading signal generation, no causal inference, no replay execution, no topology activation, no graph execution, no autonomous orchestration, no high-frequency streaming, no default Supabase persistence, and no repo writeback behavior.

## Future Recommendation
Continue bounded descriptive archetype observation over deterministic OPS-HIST-2 continuity payloads while preserving schema stability and governance boundaries.
