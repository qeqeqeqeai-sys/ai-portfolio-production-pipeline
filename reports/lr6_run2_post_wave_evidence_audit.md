## baseline comparison status
- **Direct post-wave vs pre-enrichment baseline comparison evidence is missing.**
- No inspected RUN1 artifact presents side-by-side, entity-level or topology-level delta evidence grounded in a real executed governed replay wave.
- Multiple governance artifacts explicitly state non-activation/no execution posture, which blocks empirical post-wave claims.

## weak-signal evidence audit
- OBS6 defines weak-signal candidate inclusion by design, and EXEC2 confirms weak-signal role metadata presence.
- This is **selection/scaffolding evidence**, not empirical post-wave weak-signal influence evidence.
- No inspected artifact demonstrates that weak-signal entities materially changed attribution in observed replay outcomes versus baseline.

## contradiction evidence audit
- OBS9 defines contradiction deepening criteria.
- EXEC2 and adjacent governance reports validate process readiness and artifact completeness.
- No inspected RUN1 output demonstrates measured contradiction persistence/migration/spread against baseline slices from a real executed wave.

## propagation evidence audit
- OBS9 provides propagation diversification success/failure definitions.
- OBS6 candidate design anticipates propagation bridge testing.
- Evidence of actual topology rewiring from executed post-wave outputs is not shown; available material is framework-level and template-level.

## megacap gravity audit
- OBS9 sets the weakening-of-megacap-gravity criterion.
- Inspected RUN1-related artifacts do not provide empirical pre/post gravity metrics tied to a real governed wave execution.
- Therefore, megacap gravity weakening is unproven.

## topology drift audit
- Topology drift observability is a required criterion in OBS9.
- No recurring post-wave slice evidence was found in inspected RUN1 outputs to show drift recurrence vs baseline.
- Any drift narrative would be inferential and over-claiming based on present evidence.

## replay richness audit
- RUN1 findings/reporting structure is rich in taxonomy and governance language.
- However, richer reporting structure is not equivalent to richer replay ecology.
- Without demonstrated baseline deltas from executed wave outputs, replay richness improvement is not evidenced.

## scaffold-vs-evidence assessment
- Strong evidence exists that governed process scaffolding can run deterministically and produce structured review artifacts.
- Insufficient evidence exists that replay ecology itself improved in observable, baseline-comparative terms.
- RUN1’s `STRUCTURALLY_INTERESTING_REPLAY_BEHAVIOR_OBSERVED` posture is not adequately supported by empirical deltas in inspected material.

## final audit decision
`RUN1_OVER_CREDITED_SCAFFOLDING`

## recommendation
1. Freeze any celebratory structural-improvement claims until empirical post-wave outputs are directly compared against pre-enrichment baseline outputs using OBS9 criteria.
2. Require explicit evidence tables per criterion (weak-signal attribution, contradiction migration, propagation rewiring, megacap gravity, topology drift, replay richness), each with pre/post values and recurrence checks.
3. If those baseline-linked deltas cannot be produced, downgrade continuation posture to `PROCESS_VALIDATED_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` and treat RUN1 as governance-process validation only.
