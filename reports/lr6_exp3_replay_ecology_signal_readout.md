# LR6-EXP3 Replay Ecology Signal Readout

## objective
Convert LR6-EXP2 longitudinal replay diagnostics into a deterministic, bounded, readable replay ecology interpretation layer that explains observed ecology behavior.

## methodology
- Deterministic transformation of LR6-EXP2 diagnostic metrics into six interpretation domains.
- Evidence-linked findings where each interpretation references input diagnostic values.
- Composite interpretation summary with maturity/confidence bands and bounded finding lists.
- Experimental-only certification preserving non-governed, non-persistence constraints.

## input diagnostics
- LR6-EXP2 replay drift metrics
- LR6-EXP2 propagation evolution metrics
- LR6-EXP2 contradiction ecology metrics
- LR6-EXP2 saturation and novelty metrics
- LR6-EXP2 monoculture and diversity metrics
- LR6-EXP2 ecosystem interaction metrics
- SDE-2 curated 300-stock semantic ecosystem metadata via LR6-EXP2

## replay drift readout
Interprets observed replay drift as stable/drifting/fragmenting/compressing and classifies movement as meaningful longitudinal movement versus low-signal movement.

## propagation evolution readout
Interprets pathway broadening/narrowing/concentrating/fragmenting and whether propagation structure is increasing ecological usefulness under bounded observation.

## contradiction ecology readout
Interprets contradiction emergence/persistence/migration/stabilization/decay and contradiction density sufficiency for replay ecology behavior.

## saturation/novelty readout
Interprets replay saturation behavior, novelty decay, redundancy increase, and semantic compression risk progression.

## monoculture/diversity readout
Interprets monoculture pressure, semantic gravity formation, and diversity health under anti-monoculture controls.

## ecosystem interaction readout
Interprets cross-cluster interaction density, replay cascade emergence, and ecosystem cohesion changes to assess ecology-like versus isolated behavior.

## composite replay ecology interpretation
Composite payload includes:
- dominant_replay_ecology_state
- strongest_observed_signal
- weakest_observed_signal
- replay_ecology_maturity_band
- observation_confidence_band
- key_ecological_findings
- caveats
- next_observation_priorities

## maturity assessment
Maturity is derived from deterministic weighting of interaction density, propagation structure quality, contradiction persistence, and bounded saturation/monoculture pressure.

## caveats
- Bounded deterministic slices can underrepresent low-frequency semantic transitions.
- Interpretation labels are observational categories and not optimization objectives.
- Findings are evidence-linked to LR6-EXP2 diagnostics and should be re-evaluated as new bounded slices are added.

## next observation priorities
- Track contradiction persistence versus contradiction cluster migration.
- Track propagation concentration versus fragmentation.
- Track novelty decay and replay compression for saturation inflection.
- Track cross-cluster coupling and replay cascade emergence.

## explicit experimental-only statement
LR6-EXP3 is experimental-only and bounded observation mode.

## explicit no-prediction statement
LR6-EXP3 does not generate prediction outputs.

## explicit no-trading statement
LR6-EXP3 does not generate trading outputs.

## explicit no-governed-activation statement
LR6-EXP3 does not activate governed LR6 execution paths.
