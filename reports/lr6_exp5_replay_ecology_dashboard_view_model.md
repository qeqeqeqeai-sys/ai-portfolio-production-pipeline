# LR6-EXP5 Replay Ecology Dashboard View Model

## Objective
Implement an experimental-only, deterministic replay ecology dashboard view model that converts LR6-EXP2 diagnostics, LR6-EXP3 interpretation readouts, and LR6-EXP4 evidence traces into readable operator-facing ecology-state visibility.

## Methodology
- Reused bounded deterministic payloads from LR6-EXP2, LR6-EXP3, and LR6-EXP4.
- Built sectioned panel constructors for overview, drift, propagation, contradiction, saturation/monoculture, interaction, and attribution.
- Preserved additive architecture by introducing a new module only.
- Enforced bounded output sizes for observations and attribution lists.
- Attached evidence references per panel for transparent traceability.

## Dashboard Philosophy
- Prefer ecological readability over raw metric dumps.
- Prefer concise interpretation over procedural diagnostics.
- Preserve deterministic reproducibility and experimental isolation.

## Overview Panel Design
The overview panel provides dominant replay ecology state, replay density band, replay ecology maturity band, observation confidence band, strongest observed signal, weakest observed signal, and a concise ecological caveat summary.

## Replay Drift Panel Design
The replay drift panel expresses replay drift direction, replay recurrence changes, topology stability, semantic velocity, and replay fragmentation/compression context as readable statements with evidence linkage.

## Propagation Panel Design
The propagation evolution panel summarizes strongest propagation pathways, replay bridge entities, pathway concentration zones, cross-cluster propagation structures, and propagation fragmentation observations.

## Contradiction Panel Design
The contradiction ecology panel reports strongest contradiction surfaces, contradiction persistence zones, contradiction-heavy clusters, and contradiction migration summaries.

## Saturation / Monoculture Panel Design
The saturation and monoculture panel reports replay saturation zones, novelty decay areas, semantic gravity observations, monoculture risk contributors, and replay concentration observations.

## Interaction Panel Design
The ecosystem interaction panel reports strongest interaction zones, replay cascade structures, cross-cluster coupling summaries, interaction-density observations, and replay ecology cohesion observations.

## Attribution Panel Design
The attribution panel surfaces most referenced entities, most referenced clusters, strongest replay bridge entities, strongest propagation contributors, and strongest contradiction contributors.

## Replay Ecology Readability Assessment
This implementation advances replay ecology visibility by presenting deterministic ecology-state snapshots through bounded, sectioned, evidence-linked dashboard payloads suitable for operator interpretation.

## Caveats
- Bounded deterministic slices can underrepresent low-frequency semantic transitions.
- Labels remain experimental readout categories.
- Readouts are descriptive replay ecology observations only.

## Next Observation Priorities
- Monitor contradiction persistence versus contradiction migration.
- Monitor propagation concentration versus fragmentation.
- Monitor novelty decay and replay compression for saturation inflection.
- Monitor cross-cluster coupling and replay cascades for ecology cohesion progression.

## Explicit Statements
- **Experimental-only statement:** LR6-EXP5 is experimental mode only.
- **No-prediction statement:** LR6-EXP5 provides no prediction logic.
- **No-trading statement:** LR6-EXP5 provides no trading logic.
- **No-governed-activation statement:** LR6-EXP5 does not perform governed LR6 activation.
