# LR6-EXP6A Longitudinal Snapshot Comparison

## Objective
Implement deterministic, bounded, experimental-only replay ecology snapshot comparison to detect persisted, emerged, weakened, disappeared, and intensified ecological signals over time.

## Methodology
- Normalize LR6-EXP6 payload sections into deterministic comparison-ready structures.
- Compare prior/current snapshots per domain with bounded stable term extraction.
- Build sequence comparison by deterministic pairwise progression across a bounded short sequence.
- Produce observational summaries and caveats only.

## Snapshot Comparison Philosophy
Comparison is evidence-linked and observational. It does not produce prediction, trading intent, governed activation, or portfolio guidance.

## Ecology State Comparison Design
Tracks dominant ecology state, density, maturity, confidence, strongest signal, and weakest signal using deterministic persisted/shifted/emerged/disappeared classifications.

## Replay Drift Comparison Design
Compares replay drift summaries, semantic velocity indicators, recurrence/topology stability indicators, and fragmentation/compression observations.

## Propagation Comparison Design
Compares strongest pathways, bridge entities, concentration zones, and cross-cluster propagation references.

## Contradiction Comparison Design
Compares strongest contradiction surfaces, contradiction-heavy clusters, persistence zones, and migration summaries.

## Saturation/Monoculture Comparison Design
Compares saturation zones, novelty decay areas, semantic gravity observations, concentration references, and monoculture contributors.

## Ecosystem Interaction Comparison Design
Compares interaction zones, cascade structures, coupling summaries, and entity-cluster interaction references.

## Entity/Cluster Attribution Comparison Design
Compares most-referenced entities/clusters and strongest bridge/contradiction/propagation contributors.

## Persistent/Emerged/Disappeared Signal Design
Uses deterministic bounded term differencing to surface persistent, emerged, disappeared signals, and maps emerged/disappeared sets into intensified/weakened top-level signal views.

## Boundedness Review
- Top-level signal lists are bounded to 10.
- Per-domain lists are bounded to 8.
- Caveats and next priorities are bounded to 8.
- Sequence comparison is bounded to first 4 snapshots.

## Caveats
- Missing optional sections reduce evidence density and may increase insufficient-evidence classifications.
- Comparisons remain descriptive and do not imply causality beyond observed fields.

## Next Observation Priorities
- Increase section completeness to reduce caveats.
- Observe repeated pairwise comparisons to strengthen persistence confidence.
- Track contradiction and propagation migrations for longitudinal interpretability.

This module is experimental-mode only.
