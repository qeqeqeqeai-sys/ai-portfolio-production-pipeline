# P1A — Institutional Narrative Architecture

## Executive Summary
The Structural Transmission Project has completed a certified operational baseline across O1–O10 and D1–D4. The platform now enters presentation-grade documentation mode to support institutional review, governance communication, and supervised demonstration.

The platform is a deterministic institutional intelligence system for expectation-failure interpretation. It provides structured, replayable visibility into defined transmission signals and does not perform autonomous portfolio action.

This document explains what the platform is, what it does, what it excludes, and why deterministic architecture, bounded intelligence, and certification-first operations are central to institutional credibility.

## Platform Identity
**Deterministic Institutional Expectation-Failure Intelligence Platform**

Identity principles:
- Deterministic: same certified inputs produce same outputs.
- Institutional: designed for governance, reviewability, and controlled interpretation.
- Expectation-failure focused: centered on structured detection and explanation of institutional signal deterioration.
- Intelligence platform, not execution platform: informs supervisor interpretation; does not execute financial action.

## Problem Framing
Institutions require a method to identify and communicate expectation-failure conditions before they propagate into unmanaged decision risk. Traditional ad hoc analysis often lacks replayability, certification lineage, and standardized supervisory visibility.

The platform addresses this by:
- Converting predefined market and valuation context into governed intelligence views.
- Maintaining deterministic behavior under fixed contracts.
- Providing explainable dashboard visibility and supervisory replay.
- Enforcing strict operational boundaries that prevent unauthorized behavior expansion.

## Core Architecture
The certified architecture is organized around four institutional control layers:

1. **Contract Layer**  
   Defines immutable data and guardrail interfaces (including D1G freeze), ensuring stable schema and interpretation boundaries.

2. **Deterministic Processing Layer**  
   Applies bounded intelligence logic through fixed, auditable transformations with no autonomous adaptation.

3. **Operational Visibility Layer**  
   Exposes standardized outputs through the O-series dashboard infrastructure for institutional monitoring and review.

4. **Certification & Replay Layer**  
   Provides evidence-grade reproducibility via D-series demonstration chain, including data seeding, visibility certification, supervisor playback, and demo closeout.

This architecture supports explainability and auditability without introducing autonomous control behavior.

## Deterministic Design Philosophy
Determinism is treated as an institutional control, not merely an engineering preference.

Why determinism matters:
- Enables reliable supervisory comparison across review cycles.
- Supports root-cause analysis when conditions change.
- Prevents hidden behavior drift caused by uncontrolled adaptation.
- Provides confidence that documented interpretations can be independently reproduced.

Design commitments:
- Fixed input/output contracts.
- Bounded transformation logic.
- No implicit online learning loops.
- Versioned and reviewable certification artifacts.

## Replay & Certification Methodology
Replayability and certification establish operational trust:

1. Seed known inputs (D1).
2. Freeze contracts (D1G).
3. Validate visibility correctness (D2).
4. Replay and supervisor-verify outcomes (D3).
5. Close demonstration under formal controls (D4).

This methodology ensures that explanatory claims are evidence-backed, not narrative-only assertions.

## Dashboard Visibility & Explainability
The dashboard layer serves as a controlled institutional interface:

- Presents deterministic outputs in review-friendly formats.
- Preserves traceability from displayed views back to certified data and logic boundaries.
- Supports supervisor walkthroughs without requiring direct system-level intervention.
- Emphasizes interpretation support, not autonomous recommendation authority.

Explainability posture:

- Clear boundary between observable outputs and decision authority.

## Safety Boundaries & Explicit Exclusions
The platform operates within strict bounded-intelligence constraints.

### Explicit Exclusions
The system does **not** perform:
- Trade execution.
- Autonomous investing.
- Target-price generation.
- Portfolio optimization.
- Uncontrolled LLM reasoning.
- Adaptive control-system behavior.
- Autonomous trading-agent operation.
- Probabilistic black-box orchestration.

### Boundary Controls
- Guardrail contracts define permitted behavior scope.
- Deterministic processing prevents hidden adaptive drift.
- Certification gates enforce review before any operational progression.
- Supervisor interpretation remains external to system autonomy.
