# P2 Interview Delivery Guide

## Purpose
Provide a consistent interviewer-facing delivery structure that is technically rigorous, institutionally credible, and boundary-safe.

## How to Position the System
Position as a deterministic, bounded intelligence platform for structural expectation-failure interpretation and reviewer-visible evidence trails.

## 30-Second Verbal Opening
"This is a Deterministic Institutional Expectation-Failure Intelligence Platform. It surfaces structural stress and expectation-failure context in a bounded, reviewable workflow. It is designed for explainability and governance visibility, not autonomous execution or predictive trading."

## 2-Minute Executive Explanation
- Describe the problem: institutional teams need explainable interpretation, not opaque automation.
- Describe system role: deterministic transformation of structured inputs into legible dashboard outputs.
- Describe control model: certification-backed visibility and playback traceability (D2/D3/D4).
- Describe boundary: intelligence support only; no execution, forecasting, or optimization layer.

## 5-Minute Architecture Walkthrough
1. Start with P1A narrative architecture and core system framing.
2. Use P1B/P1B1 visual architecture to explain component boundaries.
3. Explain deterministic seed and guardrail contract context (D1/D1G).
4. Reference O1–O10 dashboard certification as operational scope.
5. Close with D4 closeout as demo reliability anchor.

## 10-Minute Institutional Demonstration
- Minute 0-2: Positioning + exclusions.
- Minute 2-4: Architecture-first explanation (P1A/P1B).
- Minute 4-7: Dashboard walkthrough sequence.
- Minute 7-8: Replay and certification evidence (D2/D3).
- Minute 8-9: Demo environment confidence (D4).
- Minute 9-10: Closing and reviewer-specific follow-up path.

## How to Avoid Overclaiming
- Use verbs such as "interpret," "surface," "contextualize," and "present."
- Avoid verbs such as "predict," "optimize," "execute," and "autonomously trade."
- Tie every capability claim to a visible artifact, report, or certified scope.

## How to Explain What the System Does Not Do
State clearly:
- no autonomous trading
- no target prices
- no portfolio optimization
- no trade execution
- no uncontrolled LLM reasoning
- no adaptive control systems
- no autonomous trading agents
- no predictive market forecasting
- no buy/sell/short recommendations

## Common Interviewer Questions
1. **How is this different from a trading bot?**
   - It is bounded to interpretation and presentation; it does not execute trades or emit trading actions.
2. **How do you ensure repeatability?**
   - Deterministic sample-data seeding and certification reports provide reproducible walkthrough baselines.
3. **How do you support governance review?**
   - Artifact-centric traceability, explicit boundaries, and replay/certification visibility.
4. **What is the strongest proof of reliability?**
   - Combined O1–O10 operational certification plus D2/D3/D4 demo-readiness evidence.
5. **What is intentionally out of scope?**
   - Forecasting, autonomous decisioning, execution workflows, and optimization systems.

## Strong Closing Framing
"The platform is intentionally constrained to maximize institutional trust: deterministic behavior, explainable outputs, and certification-backed reviewer visibility. Its value is disciplined interpretation and communication, not autonomous financial action."
