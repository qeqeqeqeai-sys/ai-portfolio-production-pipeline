## objective
Design a deterministic, governance-safe, observational-only framework for SEFI Phase A1 curated ~300-stock universe construction.

## observational-only boundary
Phase A1 keeps all expansion observational-only with no ingestion, no replay operationalization, no topology activation, and no write-path/schema changes.

## universe design philosophy
The universe is generated from fixed domain allocations and deterministic entity templates, avoiding random sampling and preserving repeatability.

## sector allocation model
Allocation follows domain-first weighting, then projects into sectors/subsectors with monoculture guardrails (no single sector above 55%).

## curated universe coverage
Coverage includes AI infrastructure, semis (including analog/memory), cloud, cybersecurity, enterprise software, AI apps, robotics, automation, networking, telecom infra, cooling, power/grid, logistics, capital equipment, defense-AI, edge, cyclicals, macro-sensitive leaders, valuation/volatility extremes, contradiction-rich and regime-transition-sensitive entities.

## anti-monoculture design
Domain quotas intentionally spread representation across Information Technology, Industrials, Communication Services, Utilities, Financials, Consumer Discretionary, and multi-sector overlays.

## contradiction-richness design
Each entity includes a contradiction role and replay-richness rationale, creating structural tension surfaces and non-trivial narrative conflicts.

## regime-diversity design
Regime sensitivity tags are deterministic (high/moderate), ensuring mixed exposure to inflation, growth shocks, policy transitions, and risk-on/off rotations.

## propagation-diversity design
Propagation roles alternate between upstream shock origination and downstream amplification; topology roles alternate cluster anchors and bridges.

## governance preservation
Boundary certification explicitly disables replay scaling, topology activation, contradiction persistence migration, prediction/trading, schema expansion, and direct SQL.

## residual risks
Template-generated entities are observational placeholders and require later governance-approved curation refinement for production realism.

## recommendation for Phase A2
Proceed with additive observational scoring only after Phase A1 contracts pass and boundary flags remain unchanged.
