# LR6-EXP8 Replay Ecology Findings Report

## Objective
Implement deterministic, bounded, evidence-linked replay ecology findings synthesis from LR6-EXP7 interestingness outputs.

## Methodology
Use existing LR6-EXP2/3/4/5/6A/7 observational outputs and normalize ranked ecological changes into bounded, operator-readable finding objects.

## Findings Philosophy
The report synthesizes what changed, persisted, intensified, weakened, emerged, or disappeared across replay ecology structures without prediction or trading behavior.

## Executive Summary Design
Captures dominant replay ecology state, maturity/interestingness band, strongest persistence/emergence/weakening signals, contradiction and propagation highlights, and bounded caveats.

## Top Findings Design
Builds deterministic top findings with title, summary, interestingness band, support sets (entities/clusters/pathways/contradictions/evidence refs), structural significance, and caveats.

## Contradiction Findings Design
Highlights contradiction persistence, emerged contradiction surfaces, and contradiction-linked replay ecology shifts.

## Propagation Findings Design
Highlights replay bridge emergence, pathway concentration/diversification, and cross-cluster propagation evolution.

## Saturation/Monoculture Findings Design
Highlights semantic gravity movement, saturation pressure movement, novelty decay context, and monoculture concentration caution.

## Ecosystem Interaction Findings Design
Highlights interaction-density increase/decrease, replay cascade emergence, and ecosystem coupling changes.

## Attribution Findings Design
Highlights recurring entities, replay bridge entities, persistent/emerged clusters, and attribution shifts.

## Structural Significance Design
Deterministic significance banding (baseline/moderate/high structural significance) is derived from bounded interestingness score tiers.

## Boundedness Review
- top_interesting_findings <= 10
- findings per domain <= 8
- supporting_entities <= 8
- supporting_clusters <= 6
- supporting_pathways <= 6
- supporting_contradiction_surfaces <= 6
- supporting_evidence_refs <= 6
- caveats <= 8
- next observation priorities <= 8

## Caveats
Observational replay ecology findings can include sparse evidence zones and low-information churn; these remain reported as caveats.

## Next Observation Priorities
Track contradiction persistence, propagation evolution, semantic gravity movement, saturation pressure movement, and cross-cluster coupling continuity.

## Experimental-only Statement
This implementation is experimental-mode only.

## No-prediction Statement
This implementation does not produce predictions.

## No-trading Statement
This implementation does not produce trading outputs.

## No-governed-activation Statement
This implementation does not activate governed LR6 behavior.
