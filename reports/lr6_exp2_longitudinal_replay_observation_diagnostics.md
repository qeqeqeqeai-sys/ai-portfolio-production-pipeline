# LR6-EXP2 Bounded Longitudinal Replay Observation Diagnostics

## Objective
Implement deterministic, bounded, additive longitudinal replay ecology diagnostics for the curated 300-stock semantic ecosystem in experimental mode only.

## Methodology
- Deterministic slicing over curated 300-stock universe.
- Fixed bounded replay windows with stable slice_count and max_entities.
- Metric extraction for replay drift, propagation evolution, contradiction ecology, saturation evolution, monoculture drift, and ecosystem interaction.

## Bounded Longitudinal Design
- No unbounded history accumulation.
- No persistence writes.
- No SQL paths.
- No external API calls.

## Replay Drift Observations
- Replay drift score and topology stability index are computed from first-vs-last cluster distributions.
- Semantic velocity and recurrence decay are computed longitudinally across bounded slices.

## Propagation Evolution Observations
- Propagation entropy, pathway concentration, fragmentation, and diversity deltas are computed from pathway tags.

## Contradiction Ecology Observations
- Contradiction persistence, recurrence, and cluster drift are computed from contradiction surfaces and cluster concentrations.

## Saturation Evolution Observations
- Saturation velocity, novelty decay, and semantic compression are computed from bounded slice transitions.

## Monoculture Drift Observations
- Monoculture drift, semantic gravity, and cluster dominance shift are computed from dominant cluster dynamics.

## Ecosystem Interaction Observations
- Interaction density delta, replay cascade emergence, cohesion shift, and cross-cluster coupling are computed from propagation and contradiction adjacency proxies.
