# LR6-EXP4 Replay Ecology Evidence Trace & Entity-Level Attribution

## Objective
Implement deterministic, bounded, evidence-linked replay ecology attribution that maps LR6-EXP3 interpretation outputs and LR6-EXP2 diagnostics back to specific entities, semantic clusters, propagation pathways, contradiction surfaces, and interaction structures in the curated 300-stock semantic ecosystem.

## Methodology
- Reuse bounded longitudinal slice construction from LR6-EXP2.
- Reuse interpretation-layer maturity/confidence from LR6-EXP3.
- Build additive attribution views across six domains with deterministic sorting and fixed bounds.
- Preserve experimental isolation and no-persistence/no-SQL/no-external-API constraints.

## Attribution Philosophy
EXP4 is ecological attribution, not prediction. Outputs identify replay contributors, propagation bridges, contradiction persistence contributors, semantic gravity participants, replay density contributors, and ecological interaction nodes.

## Replay Drift Attribution
- Entity-level replay drift contributor ranking by deterministic recurrence in bounded slices.
- Cluster-level replay drift concentration references.
- Ecosystem role recurrence and pathway references linked to drift signatures.

## Propagation Pathway Attribution
- Propagation-dense entities and pathway concentration references.
- Replay bridge entities participating in chain-like propagation structures.
- Cross-cluster pathway links for topology inspection.

## Contradiction Ecology Attribution
- Contradiction persistent entities and contradiction cluster concentration references.
- Contradiction surface density and longitudinal migration references.

## Saturation / Novelty Attribution
- Replay recurrence entities and saturation pressure entities.
- Replay density references and novelty decay cluster traces.

## Monoculture / Diversity Attribution
- Replay monoculture entities and cluster concentration references.
- Semantic gravity entities and diversity decay references.

## Ecosystem Interaction Attribution
- Replay interaction entities and replay cascade references.
- Cross-cluster interaction references and ecosystem coupling entities.

## Composite Attribution Findings
Composite summary includes:
- most_referenced_entities
- most_referenced_clusters
- strongest_replay_bridge_entities
- strongest_contradiction_surfaces
- strongest_propagation_pathways
- strongest_saturation_signals
- strongest_interaction_zones
- replay_ecology_density_band
- replay_ecology_maturity_band
- observation_confidence_band
- ecological_caveats
- next_observation_priorities

## Replay Ecology Density Assessment
Density band is deterministically computed from LR6-EXP2 longitudinal replay ecology score using bounded low/moderate/high banding.

## Replay Ecology Maturity Assessment
Maturity and confidence bands are inherited from LR6-EXP3 interpretation summary to preserve interpretability continuity across phases.

## Caveats
- Bounded slices can underweight low-frequency transitions.
- Attribution is deterministic and observational, not causal proof.
- Outputs are evidence-linked diagnostics for replay topology understanding only.
