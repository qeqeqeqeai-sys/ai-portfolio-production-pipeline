# LR6-R — Replay Ecology Reactivation Readiness Plan

## Objective
Define deterministic governance, gating, diagnostics, boundedness, and safety structures required prior to any eventual LR6 replay ecology reactivation.

## LR5 → SDE → LR6-R Sequencing Interpretation
LR5 established governed replay practice. SDE-1A through SDE-1D expanded and certified semantic ecosystem quality. LR6-R is explicitly a readiness planning layer before any future LR6 replay execution consideration.

## Replay Ecology Philosophy
Replay ecology quality is prioritized over replay volume. Controlled heterogeneity, contradiction richness, and propagation richness are treated as first-class readiness constraints.

## Replay Quality vs Replay Quantity Interpretation
Higher replay counts without novelty and diversity are interpreted as ecological deterioration. Readiness therefore constrains replay quantity via bounded windows and saturation limits.

## Bounded Replay Methodology
Use bounded replay windows, dry-run-first controls, and deterministic gating prior to any execution-class decision.

## Replay Ecology Safety Methodology
Apply no-write dry-runs, escalation gates, pause conditions, rollback guidance, and longitudinal observability requirements.

## Replay Saturation Prevention Methodology
Use replay saturation limits and novelty decay floors. Trigger pause-and-escalate when limits are breached.

## Semantic Diversity Preservation Methodology
Require semantic diversity floors and cross-ecosystem participation floors to preserve ecological breadth.

## Contradiction Richness Preservation Methodology
Require contradiction density floors so replay pressure does not collapse interpretation surfaces.

## Propagation Richness Preservation Methodology
Require pathway richness and role diversity floors to preserve transmission interpretability.

## Monoculture Prevention Methodology
Apply ecosystem share caps and concentration caps to prevent theme lock-in.

## Dry-Run-First Governance Methodology
No future activation path may bypass dry-run-first evaluation and operator governance review.

## Replay Pause/Escalation Methodology
Pause immediately on saturation, diversity, contradiction, propagation, or monoculture breaches; escalate to governance operators for adjudication.

## Governance Preservation Review
All hard constraints are preserved: no replay execution, no persistence writes, no SQL, no external API calls, no predictive/trading logic, and no autonomous expansion.

## Deterministic Reproducibility Review
Deterministic seed and versioning are fixed and represented in config and payload artifacts.

## Explicit State
**LR6 is NOT reactivated.**

## Recommended Conditions for Eventual LR6 Activation
Eventual activation may be considered only if:
1. Dry-run-first gates pass.
2. Safety thresholds remain in-bounds longitudinally.
3. Governance escalation review provides explicit approval.
4. Bounded replay constraints remain active and auditable.
