# OPS-HIST-6 Regime Morphology & Structural Transition Observation

## Objective
Observe how temporal continuity regimes morph across historical period artifacts using bounded deterministic descriptive markers.

## Relationship to OPS-HIST-1/2/3/4/5
OPS-HIST-6 consumes OPS-HIST-5 temporal regime artifacts and extends descriptive observation from recurrence and regime coverage into morphology and structural transition shape summaries.

## Regime Morphology Observation Design
Morphology class is determined from observed class-change density and discontinuity markers in OPS-HIST-5 temporal regime classes.

## Transition Shape Design
Transition shape class is deterministic from observed adjacent class-change density: smooth, mixed, abrupt, or insufficient when only one artifact is available.

## Deformation Marker Design
Deformation markers are derived from observed stability and morphology drift across ordered artifacts.

## Fragmentation Propagation Design
Fragmentation propagation is derived from observed high-fragmentation regime occurrences across artifacts.

## Evidence Record Design
Each evidence record includes source indexes, date coverage, regime classes, morphology markers, and governance metadata.

## Streamlit Payload Design
Frontend-independent panels provide scorecards, sequence timeline, transition and deformation tables, discontinuity and persistence panels, evidence table, and governance boundaries.

## Canonical Table Payload Design
Flat deterministic rows provide schema-stable records for morphology scorecards, sequence rows, transition/deformation/fragmentation/stability/discontinuity/persistence rows, evidence rows, and governance rows.

## Governance Certification
OPS-HIST-6 remains observational, historical, deterministic, descriptive, and bounded with no prediction or trading execution.

## Deterministic Test Summary
Dedicated OPS-HIST-6 tests verify fail-closed behavior, schema stability, deterministic outputs, classification stability, markdown stability, payload stability, and governance boundaries.

## Explicit Forbidden Architecture Boundaries
No prediction, forecasting, trading signal generation, replay execution, topology activation, graph execution, autonomous orchestration, or streaming pathways are introduced.

## Future Recommendation
Continue deterministic morphology observation with additional bounded historical period coverage while preserving schema stability and governance boundaries.
