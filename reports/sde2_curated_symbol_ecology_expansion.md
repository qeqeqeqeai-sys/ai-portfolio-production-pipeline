# SDE-2 Curated Symbol Ecology Expansion

## Objective
Expand the observational universe from 50 symbols to a deterministic curated set of 150–300 symbols while preserving boundedness, determinism, governance integrity, and operator interpretability.

## Topology diversification rationale
The expansion is intentionally anti-monoculture: it diversifies sectors, contradictory regime proxies, macro-sensitive ETFs, defensives, and duration-sensitive entities to increase observational pathway richness.

## Category construction methodology
The universe is manually curated across 20 deterministic categories, with each category represented by explicit symbol lists and fixed ordering. No random sampling is used.

## Anti-monoculture strategy
Deterministic controls enforce maximum sector concentration, correlated-cluster ratio limits, minimum category count, and minimum contradiction diversity ratio.

## Diversity metrics
Implemented metrics:
- sector_diversity_ratio
- regime_diversity_ratio
- contradiction_diversity_ratio
- monoculture_risk_ratio
- topology_balance_ratio
- propagation_pathway_diversity_ratio

## Boundedness strategy
Universe size is bounded at 150–300. Artifact sizes are bounded and test-validated for deterministic and review-safe outputs.

## Governance posture
Certified:
- observational-only semantics
- no prediction/trading logic
- no replay activation
- no topology activation
- no autonomous orchestration
- no cognition persistence introduced

## Next-phase guidance
Use this SDE-2 curated universe as readiness input for future HIST-DENSITY planning only. Do not auto-activate ingestion runs in this phase.
