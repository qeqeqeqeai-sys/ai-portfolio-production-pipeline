# OPS-HIST-4 Archetype Persistence & Recurrence Ecology Observation

## Objective
Observe archetype persistence, recurrence density, transition behavior, and recurrence ecology across historical OPS-HIST-3 archetype artifacts in deterministic descriptive mode.

## Relationship to OPS-HIST-1/2/3
OPS-HIST-4 consumes OPS-HIST-3 archetype outputs. It does not regenerate OPS-HIST-1 snapshots, OPS-HIST-2 continuity intelligence, or OPS-HIST-3 archetype compression.

## Recurrence Ecology Design
Deterministic counts, shares, and classifications are generated from observed archetype dimension and composite labels across one or more artifacts.

## Persistence Observation Design
Persistence is measured as repeated appearances of identical dimension-archetype pairs across artifacts.

## Recurrence Classification Design
Recurrence density and persistence classes are threshold-based and deterministic.

## Evidence Record Design
Each record includes schema provenance, artifact coverage, archetype labels, recurrence/persistence counts and shares, rationale, and governance metadata.

## Streamlit Payload Design
Stable frontend-independent payload panels are produced for scorecards, tables, diversity observations, evidence rows, and governance boundaries.

## Canonical Table Payload Design
Flat deterministic row groups are emitted with explicit artifact_count, recurrence_depth, date coverage, and reviewed snapshot totals.

## Governance Certification
Observational-only, historical-only recurrence ecology mode with no prediction, no trading execution, no replay activation, no topology activation, no orchestration, and no streaming.

## Deterministic Test Summary
OPS-HIST-4 tests enforce fail-closed behavior, deterministic equality, schema stability, class stability, markdown stability, and governance boundary flags.

## Explicit Forbidden Architecture Boundaries
No prediction, forecasting, trading action generation, causality claims, replay execution, topology activation, graph execution, orchestration, streaming, Supabase writes, or repo writeback.

## Future Recommendation
Continue bounded descriptive recurrence ecology observation with deterministic schema checks as artifact depth increases.
