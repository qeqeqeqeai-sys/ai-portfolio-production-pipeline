# OPS-HIST-7 Regime Ecology Saturation & Structural Density Observation

## Objective
Observe regime ecology saturation, structural density, continuity crowding, recurrence congestion, topology concentration, and morphology diversity from OPS-HIST-6 morphology artifacts using bounded deterministic historical period observation.

## Relationship to OPS-HIST-1/2/3/4/5/6
OPS-HIST-7 consumes OPS-HIST-6 outputs and does not duplicate OPS-HIST-1 through OPS-HIST-6 logic. It is a descriptive aggregation layer across morphology artifacts.

## Ecology Saturation Design
Ecology saturation is classified deterministically from observed morphology concentration across artifacts and labeled as low, moderate, high, or concentrated ecology saturation.

## Structural Density Design
Structural density is classified from repeated morphology occupancy counts and represented as sparse, moderate, dense, or highly dense structural density.

## Continuity Crowding Design
Continuity crowding is observed from morphology reuse relative to diversity and represented as low, moderate, or high continuity crowding.

## Topology Concentration Design
Topology concentration is observed from transition shape concentration and represented as distributed, mixed, concentrated, or topology monoculture.

## Evidence Record Design
Each evidence row includes schema identifiers, source artifact index, bounded snapshot dates, morphology classes, saturation markers, descriptive rationale, and governance metadata.

## Streamlit Payload Design
The Streamlit payload is frontend-independent and schema-stable, exposing saturation scorecard, density timeline, crowding/congestion/diversity/concentration panels, evidence table, and governance boundary panel.

## Canonical Table Payload Design
Canonical table rows are deterministic and flat, include explicit artifact coverage and saturation depth context, and support tabular exports without excessive nesting.

## Governance Certification
OPS-HIST-7 remains observational-only and historical-only with explicit non-activation of replay, topology activation, graph execution, orchestration, streaming, Supabase writes, and repo writeback.

## Deterministic Test Summary
Tests validate fail-closed input behavior, schema stability, deterministic output equality, single/multi-artifact depth handling, classification stability, payload schema stability, markdown stability, governance boundary presence, and forbidden vocabulary controls.

## Explicit Forbidden Architecture Boundaries
No prediction, no trading execution, no replay execution, no topology activation, no graph execution engines, no autonomous orchestration, no streaming, no Supabase persistence, and no repo writeback.

## Future Recommendation
Continue bounded deterministic ecology saturation observation with incremental descriptive coverage expansion while preserving strict governance boundaries.
