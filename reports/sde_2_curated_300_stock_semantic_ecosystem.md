# SDE-2 Curated 300-Stock Semantic Ecosystem

## objective
Build a deterministic, additive, experimental-mode-only 300-stock semantic ecosystem for replay propagation, contradiction, recurrence, and longitudinal ecology observation.

## methodology
- Built a deterministic 10-cluster curated stock universe in `curated_stock_universe.py`.
- Preserved additive architecture via pure in-memory loaders/validators.
- Added deterministic validation APIs and deterministic tests.
- Used fixture-based FMP coverage validation (`FMP_COVERAGE_FIXTURE`) with no live API dependency.

## ecosystem philosophy
The universe is curated for semantic interaction density, contradiction richness, and propagation pathway diversity rather than index replication or predictive optimization.

## sector distribution
Coverage spans Technology, Utilities, Energy, Industrials, Financials, Consumer Discretionary, Healthcare, Materials, and Communication/RealAssets.

## semantic-cluster distribution
10 semantic clusters are represented with anti-monoculture guardrail checks to prevent dominant concentration.

## contradiction-surface diversity
Core contradiction surfaces include AI exuberance, enterprise spending compression, rates sensitivity, geopolitical exposure, consumer weakness, valuation excess, inventory cyclicality, liquidity fragility, and infrastructure bottlenecks.

## propagation-pathway diversity
Propagation tags include AI compute chain, semiconductor capex chain, enterprise IT spending, power demand chain, energy inflation transmission, credit stress transmission, industrial automation chain, freight/logistics chain, consumer discretionary chain, and healthcare innovation chain.

## anti-monoculture review
Cluster share validation enforces no single cluster above configured concentration thresholds during deterministic checks.

## replay-density assessment
The cluster design creates dense cross-regime observational surfaces across growth, defensive, cyclical, macro-sensitive, and stress-sensitive cohorts.

## saturation-risk assessment
Cluster-level caps and diversity checks reduce semantic monoculture and lower replay saturation risk.

## replay ecology readiness assessment
SDE-2 provides sufficient semantic density and contradiction coverage to begin meaningful longitudinal replay ecology observation and interaction diagnostics.

## FMP coverage validation approach
FMP validation is fixture-based in deterministic tests (`validate_fmp_symbol_coverage`) and intentionally avoids live API calls in test-critical pathways.
