# P3-F Asymmetry Regime Classification Report

## Objective
Implement a deterministic, replay-safe, additive-only P3-F module that consolidates P3-A through P3-E signals into bounded structural asymmetry regimes for institutional dashboard/report interpretation.

## Scope
- Deterministic consolidation of P3-A/P3-B/P3-C/P3-D/P3-E evidence.
- Bounded 0-100 regime dimensions.
- Fixed-threshold, fixed tie-break regime classification.
- Deterministic explainability templates.
- Certification status with governance gates.

## Non-goals
- No buy/sell/hold guidance.
- No trade execution or portfolio allocation.
- No return prediction or alpha optimization.
- No stochastic/ML-adaptive/autonomous strategy behavior.

## Architectural placement
P3-F sits after P3-E and before any narrative/presentation-only layer. It is a deterministic interpretation layer that emits bounded regime envelopes.

## Relationship to P3-A through P3-E
- P3-A: resilience/fragility foundations.
- P3-B: downside/upside structural asymmetry.
- P3-C: benchmark-relative divergence pressure.
- P3-D: persistence/acceleration/compression/exhaustion context.
- P3-E: concentration, breadth, and imbalance severity context.

## Regime evidence methodology
Evidence summary combines resilience-fragility balance, benchmark divergence pressure, and imbalance severity from bounded deterministic dimensions.

## Regime pressure methodology
Pressure summary consolidates persistence, acceleration, compression, and exhaustion as transition-relevant bounded pressures.

## Transition methodology
Transition summary focuses on concentration versus breadth pressure and deterministic transition-gap magnitude.

## Classification methodology
Classification uses fixed thresholds and deterministic tie-break ordering, prioritizing extreme imbalance and systemic deterioration before lower-priority stable/compression/exhaustion regimes.

## Explainability methodology
Deterministic templates provide:
- dominant regime driver
- resilience/fragility balance
- benchmark divergence context
- persistence/acceleration context
- concentration/breadth context
- bounded structural regime label

## Certification gates
- deterministic replay
- checksum stability
- bounded scores
- valid P3-F regime
- explanation completeness
- additive-only integration
- immutability
- missing prior-layer degraded behavior
- no prediction/execution/optimization/stochastic behavior
- forbidden capability exclusion

## Governance boundaries
P3-F is interpretation-only and never emits trading, optimization, execution, or autonomous strategy directives.

## Forbidden capabilities
- buy/sell/hold
- go long/go short
- trade signal/execution
- portfolio allocation
- expected return/prediction
- alpha optimization
- leverage logic
- stochastic/adaptive ML weighting
- autonomous strategy selection

## Final interpretation
P3-F outputs deterministic structural regime classification for institutional interpretation while preserving bounded, explainable, additive-only governance constraints.
