# P1C — Interviewer Q&A Pack

## Q1: What is the platform, in one sentence?
It is a deterministic, replayable decision-support platform that translates structured narrative-risk signals into explainable institutional outputs under strict governance boundaries.

## Q2: How is this different from an AI trading bot?
A trading bot acts. This system explains. It has no trade execution authority, no autonomous investing loop, and no optimization mandate.

## Q3: What problem does it solve for institutions?
It standardizes how expectation failure and fragility signals are communicated, reducing interpretation drift and improving committee-grade consistency.

## Q4: Why emphasize deterministic architecture?
Determinism makes results reproducible, reviewable, and certifiable—core requirements in governed environments.

## Q5: How do you demonstrate reliability?
Through replayability and certification evidence: O1–O10 for operational readiness and D1–D4 for demonstration stability.

## Q6: Where does AI fit in the design?
AI is bounded to constrained interpretation roles. It does not control execution, adapt strategy autonomously, or operate outside fixed contracts.

## Q7: What are the governance safeguards?
- Contract-bounded workflows
- Explicit role separation
- Human decision accountability
- Reproducible evidence trails
- Scope-limited outputs

## Q8: What is the highest-risk misunderstanding?
Assuming it is an autonomous alpha engine. It is not; it is a controlled communication and interpretation system for institutional workflows.

## Q9: How would a portfolio manager use it?
As a structured briefing layer before human judgment: to review fragility context, expectation-failure signals, and consistent explanatory artifacts.

## Q10: What is the most important design principle?
Bounded intelligence: useful assistance within deterministic and governance-safe limits.

## Q11: What is this system NOT?
- Not a trade execution platform
- Not an autonomous investor
- Not a target-price engine
- Not a portfolio optimizer
- Not an uncontrolled LLM agent framework

## Q12: How do you present this in an interview without hype?
Lead with architecture discipline, governance fit, replay evidence, and explicit exclusions. Avoid prediction claims and autonomy language.
