# SDE-1 Curated Semantic Ecosystem Blueprint

## Strategic rationale
SEFI's structural objective in SDE-1 is to replace replay-centric breadth expansion with interpretable semantic ecosystem coverage. The design emphasizes deterministic, curated topology over uncontrolled universe growth, preserving governance boundaries and reducing false novelty from random entity inclusion.

## LR5/LR6/SDE sequencing interpretation
- **LR5** established governed replay wave discipline and constraint compliance.
- **LR6** expanded replay ecology under controlled novelty and anti-fragility controls.
- **SDE-1** pivots from replay mechanics toward ecosystem semantics: topology quality, contradiction mapping, and propagation interpretability become first-class curation primitives.

## Semantic ecosystem philosophy
1. Structural signals should be explainable through ecosystem roles.
2. Candidate inclusion should maximize pathway coverage, not symbol count.
3. Contradiction surfaces should be explicit and testable.
4. Diversity must be intentionally engineered to avoid monoculture and hype lock-in.

## Ecosystem taxonomy definitions
Twelve ecosystem families are encoded as deterministic taxonomy anchors:
1. AI compute core
2. Hyperscaler/cloud demand
3. Semiconductor supply chain
4. Power/grid/cooling/data-centre infrastructure
5. Memory/networking/interconnect
6. AI application/software monetization
7. Automation/robotics/industrial AI
8. Energy/commodities/physical inputs
9. Financing/rates/liquidity
10. China/geopolitical/export-control exposure
11. End-market demand validation
12. Narrative/hype/speculation amplification

## Structural interaction framework
- Canonical cross-ecosystem pathways are explicitly declared.
- Each entity has one primary ecosystem and two deterministic secondary ecosystems.
- Propagation links are deterministic and reproducible, enabling stable graph topology checks.

## Contradiction-topology framework
Contradiction surfaces are standardized to:
- valuation vs cashflow
- capacity vs utilization
- guidance vs orderbook
- policy vs execution

Each candidate carries at least two contradiction surfaces, enabling longitudinal stress interpretation and anomaly localization without prediction logic.

## Propagation-topology framework
Propagation roles are standardized to:
- upstream source
- midstream router
- downstream receiver
- feedback emitter

Entity-level propagation links define deterministic adjacent stress-transfer paths for replay ecology diagnostics.

## Curated ecosystem categories
The taxonomy separates demand, supply, infrastructure, financing, geopolitics, and narrative amplification layers so the system can detect cross-layer mismatches rather than over-indexing on any single thematic cluster.

## Candidate universe methodology
- Target size: ~350 entities for first curated universe.
- Deterministic ID and symbol assignment.
- Structured metadata for role, contradiction, and regime dimensions.
- Inclusion reason required per candidate.

## Ecosystem diversity methodology
- Round-robin deterministic primary ecosystem assignment.
- Secondary ecosystem assignment offsets enforce cross-family connectivity.
- Diversity checks ensure no ecosystem dominates the universe.

## Anti-monoculture methodology
- Enforced cap policy in tests: no ecosystem exceeds 20% of candidate universe.
- Mandatory cross-ecosystem interaction pathways.
- Contradiction-surface breadth required across all candidates.

## Anti-random-scaling methodology
- No random sampling.
- Deterministic seed declaration and stable topology rules.
- Fixed schema fields and candidate bounds.

## Candidate universe sizing rationale
- ~350 candidates balances semantic breadth with manual curation tractability.
- Supports later pruning toward ~300 without collapsing ecosystem coverage.
- Leaves controlled buffer for removing low-information or redundant nodes.

## Expected replay ecology benefits
- Higher signal triangulation quality through explicit pathway coverage.
- Lower replay noise from random entities.
- Better contradiction detection and propagation attribution.

## Expected longitudinal intelligence benefits
- Improved temporal comparability due to stable taxonomy and metadata.
- Better causal traceability from regime exposure + contradiction surfaces.
- Stronger governance-ready explanation artifacts.

## Governance preservation review
This design introduces **planning/config/report/test artifacts only**. It does not add replay execution, persistence workflows, SQL write/read logic, or governance bypasses.

## Deterministic reproducibility review
- Deterministic identifiers and ecosystem assignment.
- Stable schema contract.
- Fixed topology linkage pattern.
- Reproducibility tests enforce size and balance constraints.

## Recommendation for eventual SDE-1C pruning toward ~300 entities
For SDE-1C, prune ~50 entities by deterministic scoring using:
1. Lowest information_quality_score bucket first.
2. Within-bucket redundancy by near-identical role + contradiction signatures.
3. Preserve minimum per-ecosystem floor and pathway connectivity constraints.
4. Keep contradiction and regime coverage invariant before/after pruning.
